## DEV-010 planetary criteria and research clearing-mass model

The planetary-criteria panel links the [IAU's adopted 2006 Solar-System definition](https://www.iau.org/IAU/Iau/News/PR2006/iau-2006-general-assembly-resolution-votes.aspx) and [NASA's recognized dwarf-planet list](https://science.nasa.gov/dwarf-planets/) to eight planets, five recognized dwarf planets and four candidates already in the project. The definition is not applied as a failed test to satellites, the Sun, brown dwarfs or extrasolar planets. Classification-based equilibrium-shape entries are not new hydrostatic-equilibrium measurements; a candidate's radius or displayed sphere does not establish that criterion. Orbital clearing means dynamical dominance rather than a vacuum or the conversion of all nearby bodies into satellites.

The separately labelled numerical model cites [Margot (2015), AJ 150 185](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-6256/150/6/185) and [Margot, Gladman and Yang (2024), PSJ](https://doi.org/10.3847/PSJ/ad55f3). With a solar-mass host, a ten-billion-year time horizon and C=2√3, the adopted formula is m_clear/M⊕ = 0.001239(a/au)^(9/8). Pi is computed by dividing the adopted primary mass by that value, without mixing the independently rounded coefficient 807. This research diagnostic is not an IAU-adopted numerical rule, an existence probability, a measured cleared fraction, or a new official classification. Applying a circular-orbit relation at Sedna's high-eccentricity semimajor axis supplies a circular-reference value, not a validated eccentric-orbit requirement. Other planets, resonances and full dynamical history are not newly integrated.

Orbital elements and masses retain their source units, epoch and definitions. Existing Horizons responses and their preserved JPL physical table are reused alongside [JPL SBDB](https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/sbdb.html) responses. The JPL major-planet and Pluto mass-table scales differ and are not interchanged. Primary masses, unknown primary masses and combined satellite-system masses are separate. The adopted Haumea primary mass references [Ragozzine and Brown (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-6256/137/6/4766); Eris-system mass references [Holler et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2020.114130); Gonggong-system mass references [Kiss et al. An unacquired primary mass in the adopted inputs does not imply that the entire literature has never measured it.
